# Nature

For Nature and the Nature research journals.

## Page

Figures print 89 mm wide (one column) or 183 mm (two columns), and at most
170 mm tall so the legend fits beneath. Size each figure to its final width:
`Figure(width=theme.page["column"], height=...)`.

## Type

All text in a sans-serif face, preferably Helvetica or Arial; TeX Gyre Heros is
a free Helvetica clone. Every font between 5 pt and 7 pt at final size: 7 pt for
labels, 6 pt for ticks and legends, never below 5 pt. Panel letters are 8 pt
bold lowercase.

## Lines

Fine lines suit the small type: 0.75 pt for data, 0.5 pt for frames, and 0.25 pt
as the thinnest line that reproduces.

## Sources

Check the current guidelines before submission; journals revise them.
