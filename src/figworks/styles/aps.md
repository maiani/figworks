# APS: Physical Review

For the American Physical Society's journals: Physical Review Letters, the
Physical Review series, and PRX.

## Page

Plan figures for the 8.6 cm column (3 3/8 in). Wider figures may span 1.5 or 2
columns; two columns with the gutter is the 17.8 cm (7 in) text width.

## Type

Lettering at least 2 mm tall at final size, 1.5 mm for super- and subscripts,
which 7–8 pt meets: 8 pt for labels, 7 pt for ticks and legends.

The journals prescribe no typeface. This style sets figures the way a physics
paper sets its text: TeX Gyre Termes, a free Times clone that matches the
journals' body type, with math in its italic, so a symbol in a figure looks like
the same symbol in the text. Panels are lettered (a), (b), in roman, as the
captions cite them.

## Axes

Ticks point inward on all four sides, with minor ticks, the frame physicists
read values off. Keep a panel without a quantitative axis free of them.

## Lines

Curves and lines of consistent weight, at least 0.18 mm (0.5 pt) at final size,
so even the hairline here is 0.5 pt.
