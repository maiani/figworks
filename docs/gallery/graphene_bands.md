# Dirac electrons in graphene

*Condensed matter.* The honeycomb lattice, its two tight-binding bands touching at the Dirac points, the band structure along high-symmetry lines, and the density of states.

![Dirac electrons in graphene](../images/gallery-graphene_bands.svg)

**What it shows of FigWorks.** Two VecView scenes, a lattice and a band surface, beside Matplotlib panels that share the energy axis.

**Requires** VecView and TeX.

```bash
python examples/graphene_bands.py
```
