# Reaction paths on a potential-energy surface

*Chemistry.* The Müller-Brown surface in 3D, its minimum-energy path, and the barriers along the reaction coordinate.

![Reaction paths on a potential-energy surface](../images/gallery-energy_landscape.svg)

**What it shows of FigWorks.** A Matplotlib contour map rendered into a plane of a VecView scene with `fill_plane`, under the surface it maps.

**Requires** VecView and TeX.

```bash
python examples/energy_landscape.py
```

[Python source](https://github.com/maiani/figworks/blob/main/examples/energy_landscape.py) · [SVG](../images/gallery-energy_landscape.svg)
