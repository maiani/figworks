# IEEE

For IEEE transactions, journals, and conference proceedings.

## Page

One column is 3.5 in (88.9 mm, 21 picas); two columns are 7.16 in (182 mm,
43 picas).

## Type

8 pt type throughout, in Times New Roman or Arial; TeX Gyre Termes is a free
Times clone that matches IEEE body text. Nothing below 6 pt, or it becomes
illegible in print.

## Lines

Line art reproduces at 600 dpi, so keep even hairlines at 0.5 pt.

## Sources

- [IEEE Author Center: improve your
  graphics](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/improve-your-graphics/)
- [Proceedings of the IEEE: guidelines for figures and
  tables](https://proceedingsoftheieee.ieee.org/resources/guidelines-for-figures-and-tables/)

Check the current guidelines before submission; journals revise them.
