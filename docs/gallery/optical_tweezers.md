# Stretching DNA with optical tweezers

*Biophysics.* A DNA molecule held between two trapped beads, its worm-like-chain force-extension curve, and the Brownian motion that calibrates the trap.

![Stretching DNA with optical tweezers](../images/gallery-optical_tweezers.svg)

**What it shows of FigWorks.** Spheres, cones, and a helix in VecView beside simulated measurements and fits in Matplotlib.

**Requires** VecView and TeX.

```bash
python examples/optical_tweezers.py
```
